Getting Started

This app trains and tests one-class time series anomaly detection models. These models learn normal behavior only—labels are ignored during training. Use labeled data later for evaluation, not training.

Workflow Steps

  1. Prepare Your Data – Load and explore your time series to ensure it represents normal behavior.
  2. Train and Tune Models – Select models from the gallery, configure parameters, and train. Review validation metrics and adjust settings until performance meets your needs.
  3. Test and Evaluate – Apply the trained detector to test data. Use labeled data (if available) to measure accuracy and compare models.

Note: This app is not for classification or forecasting. All models assume training data represents normal system behavior.
